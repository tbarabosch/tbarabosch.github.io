The last hours before an architecture exam are a bad time to rediscover whether a standby serves reads or which endpoint avoids a NAT gateway. I condensed the distinctions I wanted in front of me into a printable one-page [AWS SAA-C03 Final Review Guide](/assets/files/aws-saa-c03-final-review-guide.pdf).

The guide focuses on service boundaries, abbreviations, current limits, resilience and cost tradeoffs, and the traps hidden in plausible answers. It is for a final pass after hands-on preparation, not a replacement for the [official AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-associate-03/solutions-architect-associate-03.html), whose objectives and service scope can change.
