I prepared this VirusTotal Hunting Cheat Sheet as a compact reference for working with VirusTotal Intelligence through the WebGUI. It collects useful search modifiers for files, URLs, domains and IP addresses, together with similarity keys, relationship pivots, ready-to-paste hunts and a practical hunting workflow.

Practical knowledge is most valuable when it is shared with the security community, and this cheat sheet should be useful to other analysts as well. Whether you need to refresh your memory or quickly check a field during a hunt, you can [download the VirusTotal Hunting Cheat Sheet (PDF)](/assets/files/virus-total-hunting-cheat-sheet.pdf).
